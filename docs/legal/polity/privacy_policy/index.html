Polity Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Baye Labs Inc.

We collect the least personal information we need to run Polity. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else handles it and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. Who we are and what this covers

1.1 Polity is run by Baye Labs Inc. ("Baye", "we", "us") in Halifax, Nova Scotia.

1.2 This policy covers the Polity website at polity.baye.ca and the Polity connector at mcp.polity.baye.ca. It applies whether or not you subscribe, including if you only sign in.

1.3 It does not cover the AI assistant you connect to Polity, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot. Your conversations with it are handled under its provider's own privacy policy.

2. Who is responsible

Our Privacy Officer is responsible for how we handle personal information. Contact: Privacy Officer, Baye Labs Inc., 1800 Argyle Street, Suite 801, Halifax, Nova Scotia B3J 3N8, Canada · support@baye.ca.

4. What we don't see or keep

  • Your conversation with your AI assistant. We never see it. It stays between you and your AI assistant's provider.
  • What your AI assistant sends to Polity. When it uses Polity, it sends a request, such as a bill number, the name of an Act or a few search words. We use the request to answer and don't keep it. Our usage records note only which tool was called, when, and whether it worked.
  • Your card number. We never receive it. Stripe holds your card details.
  • Tracking. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores only what keeps you signed in and a few display settings, such as whether a panel is open.

5. Consent

5.1 By signing in or subscribing, you consent to our collecting and using your information as this policy describes.

5.2 You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking us to close your account (section 10). We can't provide Polity without the information in section 3, so withdrawing consent ends your access.

6. Who else handles your information

6.1 We share personal information only with service providers that help us run Polity, and only for that purpose:

  • Google Cloud and Firebase: hosting, storage and sign-in;
  • Microsoft: sign-in, if you choose it;
  • Stripe: payments, billing and tax.

6.2 We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to a buyer of our business, who would have to keep protecting it under this policy.

6.3 We do not sell personal information.

7. Where your information is kept

We store Polity's data with Google Cloud in Montréal, Canada. Sign-in (Google, Microsoft) and payments (Stripe) are also processed outside Canada, including in the United States. Information held there is subject to that country's laws, and its authorities may be able to access it.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you close it, we delete or anonymize your information. The exception is what the law requires us to keep, such as billing and tax records, which we keep for the period the law sets.

9. Security

9.1 We protect personal information with safeguards suited to its sensitivity. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only people at Baye who need access to run Polity have it.

9.2 If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you and report it to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as the law requires. Where Québec law applies, we will also report it to the Commission d'accès à l'information.

10. Your rights

10.1 You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Write to our Privacy Officer at support@baye.ca. We will answer within 30 days, and we may ask you to confirm your identity first.

10.2 If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In Québec, you can also complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information.

11. Children

Polity is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with its effective date. If a change is material, we will also email subscribers at least 30 days before it takes effect.
